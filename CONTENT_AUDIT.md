# Chhattisgarh presentation: content audit

## What the current deck is doing

The presentation began as a 13-slide academic survey. The interactive version includes that survey, supplemental state statistics, a rural/urban indicator comparison, a proposed innovation roadmap, and a references page. It still mixes general background, secondary research, and proposed fieldwork, so the slides now label the evidence type and dates more clearly.

The project folder contains no village interview notes, household survey results, service-access measurements, or team field photographs. The deck should not present those as findings until the team supplies them.

## Recommended story

1. Introduce the state and locate it in central India.
2. Explain how landscape, population, livelihoods, and public services vary by place.
3. Show what the available statewide evidence does and does not establish.
4. Use rural-versus-urban indicators to make the development question concrete.
5. Turn the evidence into field questions, practical recommendations, and a measurable pilot.
6. Keep the existing survey conclusion as a summary of the source survey; the evidence pages follow it, with references last.

## Revisions made in this pass

- Replaced the draft reminders on slide 13 with a transparent scope statement and field questions. No field results were invented.
- Updated forest cover to the Forest Survey of India’s 2023 state report: 55,811.75 km², or 41.28% of the state’s geographical area.
- Corrected tin from “100% of reserves” to the Indian Bureau of Mines’ report that Chhattisgarh accounts for about 36% of national tin-ore resources. The same report gives about 20% for iron-ore resources and 18% for coal resources; these are resource shares, not production shares.
- Removed a causal claim about the sex ratio and removed an undated 2024 population projection from a Census 2011 card.
- Added a rural/urban multidimensional-poverty comparison from NITI Aayog’s 2023 review. The source reports 2015–16 and 2019–21 survey periods, not current 2026 conditions.
- Replaced generic source-portal links with the primary publications used for the updated figures.
